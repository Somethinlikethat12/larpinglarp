THE PILGRIM'S CATECHISM
LEARN THE RHYTHM.

STRIKE toward your cursor. Your blade cleaves a short arc; it is not a machine gun. Face the threat and commit to the swing.

DODGE with Space. A well-timed roll slips through attacks, but consumes Resolve. Resolve returns when you land a strike.

PARRY with Shift just before an enemy's blow lands. A successful parry staggers nearby enemies and restores Resolve. A missed parry leaves you open.

READ THE ENEMY. The Hollow rushes. The Bellbearer winds up a crushing strike. The Cantor marks the floor before calling down a burst. Clear each sanctum to choose a relic. Every fourth sanctum ends at a boss.
